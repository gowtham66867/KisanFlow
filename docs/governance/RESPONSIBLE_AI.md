# Responsible AI and DPDP Readiness

KisanFlow follows five operating principles: explicit purpose, minimum necessary data, uncertainty made visible, accountable human action and contestability. The prototype maps these to India’s Digital Personal Data Protection framework but does not claim legal certification.

## Consent receipt

Every run records data category, purpose, source, language, timestamp, expiry and revocation state. Financial activity, market signals and climate signals are separately selectable. Essential case review remains available when optional signals are refused; the UI explains the resulting confidence impact.

## Rights and grievance path

The partner institution must provide access/correction, withdrawal, deletion subject to lawful retention, nomination where applicable, and grievance channels. The action card displays a reason code and a plain-language explanation; the officer can correct data, override with a reason, or route to manual review.

## Decision accountability

KisanFlow predicts, explains and recommends. The maker prepares a proposed response and a checker approves it. The system does not autonomously change credit terms or communicate with a customer. A consent withdrawal or global kill switch prevents new agent runs.

## Pilot gates

1. Data-protection impact assessment and partner legal sign-off.
2. Retrospective temporal validation with real, de-identified partner data.
3. Threshold review by cohort and false-alert workload assessment.
4. Red-team tests for prompt injection, tool misuse and PII leakage.
5. Field-officer training, customer notice and grievance workflow.
6. Pre-agreed stop/rollback thresholds and incident owner.
