# KisanFlow Forecast + Early-Warning Model Card

## Status and intended use

`prototype_evaluation_on_synthetic_data` — version 1.0.0.

KisanFlow estimates the next 26 weeks of enterprise net cash flow as P10/P50/P90 ranges and raises an early-warning signal before a labelled stress event. It is decision support for authorised rural-finance staff. It must never sanction, reject, price, restructure, disburse, or contact a customer by itself.

## Model and evaluation

- Quantile forecasts: three `GradientBoostingRegressor` models for P10, P50 and P90.
- Baseline: same-week prior-year seasonal naïve estimate.
- Evaluation: three rolling origins, each with an eight-week holdout; no random train/test split.
- Forecast measures: MAE, sMAPE, MASE, interval coverage and interval width.
- EWS measures: precision, recall, false alerts per 100 assessments and median lead weeks.
- Fairness diagnostic: precision and recall by enterprise cohort plus maximum recall gap.

The canonical, machine-readable results are generated by `ml/train_model.py` into `artifacts/model_metrics.json`. All reported prototype metrics must come from that file.

## Inputs

Only consented, purpose-limited enterprise signals are eligible: weekly inflows/outflows, transaction counts, market index, rainfall index, and enterprise type. Protected characteristics, contact lists, message content, precise continuous location, religion, caste and political affiliation are prohibited inputs.

## Human control

An alert creates an action card with reasons and provenance. A maker may prepare it; a separate checker must approve any customer-facing or account-changing action. Withdrawing consent disables future retrieval and recommendation refresh. Historical audit records are retained only under the partner institution's lawful retention policy.

## Known limitations and prohibited claims

- Evaluation data is synthetic; results are engineering evidence, not production performance.
- Scenario forecasts are illustrative and not a promise of enterprise outcomes.
- Model quantiles are not guaranteed confidence intervals.
- Synthetic subgroup checks do not establish real-world fairness.
- No pilot should start without retrospective validation, legal review, data-protection impact assessment, approved operating thresholds and rollback criteria.

## Monitoring and rollback

Monitor missingness, feature drift, interval coverage, false-alert burden, cohort gaps, intervention acceptance and adverse outcomes. Trigger review if interval coverage changes by more than 10 percentage points, any cohort recall gap exceeds 0.15, false alerts exceed the approved threshold, or a prohibited autonomous action is attempted. Roll back to the last approved model artifact; the harness kill switch disables agent-generated action cards without disabling read-only case review.
